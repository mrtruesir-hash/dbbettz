Withdraw

How to withdraw from DBBet, step by step

Before your first withdrawal, make sure two things are in place: you have bet your deposits, and your account is verified. Then:

  1. Complete your profile. Full name, date of birth and address, plus a confirmed phone number.
  2. Check that you have bet your deposits. Your total stakes should at least equal your total deposits, at odds of 1.1 or higher.
  3. Open the cashier and choose Withdraw.
  4. Pick the same method you deposited with. If you deposited with M-Pesa, withdraw to M-Pesa on the same number.
  5. Enter the amount and confirm the request. The minimum and maximum are shown in the cashier.
  6. Track the request. Check its status in your transaction history. Do not send a second request while the first one is still being processed.

Withdrawal rules

Same method. Money is paid to the method and details you deposited with. If you used more than one method, the payout is split in proportion to what you deposited with each. The payment method must be in your own name; using someone else's wallet can be treated as fraud.
  • Bet each deposit once. Your total stakes must reach at least your total deposits, at odds of 1.1 or higher. Low-risk bets, such as covering both sides in roulette, do not count.
  • Verification (KYC) up to 72 hours. DBBet can ask for documents to verify you. The check takes up to 72 hours from when the documents are received.
  • Documents. DBBet may ask for a photo ID, proof of address or proof of your payment method. Send withdrawal documents within one month of the request, or the withdrawal can be suspended.
  • Active bonus. If you have a bonus you have not finished wagering, withdrawing before its terms are met is allowed only in limited cases.
  • No cash or cheques. All payments go through the cashier.

Pay safely

One rule stops most scams: only pay to the details shown inside your DBBet cashier at the moment you deposit.

  • Never pay anyone who contacts you on WhatsApp or Telegram with a number to pay to, even if they say they are from DBBet.
  • Do not send money to a number you saw in a picture, a group or an old message. Start every deposit fresh from the cashier.
  • Never share your wallet PIN, password or SMS code with anyone. A transaction ID is safe to share with support; your PIN never is.
  • If you think your account or wallet has been accessed by someone else, write to security@db-bet-team.com. For payment questions, write to info-en@db-bet-team.com.
  • Set a daily, weekly or monthly deposit limit on your account. Lowering a limit takes effect at once, and raising it has a 7-day wait. Read more on Responsible gaming.
FAQ

Deposit and withdrawal questions

My deposit is not showing in my balance. What should I do?
First check whether your wallet sent a confirmation SMS. If there is no SMS, the payment did not go through and the money is still in your wallet. If you have the SMS, contact support with the transaction ID, the amount, the time of payment and your registered phone number. Every step is in our deposit-not-showing guide.
How long does a DBBet withdrawal take?
Withdrawal requests are processed 24/7, and the payout can take up to 7 business days depending on the payment method. If DBBet asks for verification documents, the check takes up to 72 hours from when the documents are received.
Are there fees to deposit or withdraw?
It depends on the method. DBBet's terms say it usually covers payment-system charges, but it may decide not to. Crypto carries a service fee. The fee for your method is shown in the cashier before you confirm.
Which method is the fastest?
There is no single answer. Deposit times depend on the payment provider, and mobile-money networks can slow down at busy times, such as just before a big match. Use the wallet you use every day and keep enough balance in it, then check the time shown in the cashier.
What is the minimum deposit?
The minimum and maximum are shown in the cashier. Check the amount there before you send money. Bonuses have their own deposit terms, and the TZS minimum for a bonus is shown in your DBBet account.
