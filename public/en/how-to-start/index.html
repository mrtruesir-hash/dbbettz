How to register on DBBet by phone

The simplest way is with your phone number.

  1. Tap Register on this page to open the DBBet sign-up page.
  2. Choose By phone and enter your phone number. Check the country and account currency before you continue.
  3. In the Promo code field (optional), enter DBTZ. See our promo code page.
  4. Enter the SMS code sent to your phone to confirm the number.
  5. Log in and complete your profile: full name as on your ID, date of birth and home address.
  6. If you want a bonus, tick the option to take part in bonus offers, then choose your bonus before your first deposit.

DBBet also has One-click registration. If you use it, confirm your phone number later: bonuses need a confirmed number and a complete profile.

Sports welcome bonus: 100% on your first deposit. The TZS maximum is shown in your DBBet account. The wagering terms and time limit are on our Bonuses page.

Good to know before you register

  • 18+ only. You must be 18 or older.
  • One account only: per person, family, address, email, IP or payment method. Extra accounts can be closed.
  • Real details: your name and date of birth must match your ID, as DBBet can ask for documents before a withdrawal.
  • Your own wallet: use a mobile-money number in your name. Using someone else's can be treated as fraud.

Deposit

How to deposit on DBBet with mobile money

DBBet Tanzania accepts four mobile wallets: Mixx by Yas (Tigo Pesa), Airtel Money, Vodacom M-Pesa and HaloPesa. The steps are the same for all of them.

  1. Log in to your account and tap Deposit (the cashier).
  2. Choose your wallet: Mixx by Yas, Airtel Money, M-Pesa or HaloPesa.
  3. Enter the amount and the wallet number registered in your name. The minimum and maximum are shown in the cashier.
  4. Confirm. A wallet prompt appears on your phone: enter your PIN to approve the payment.
  5. Check your balance. It updates once the payment is confirmed; timing depends on the payment provider.

Withdraw

How to withdraw from DBBet

Withdrawals go back to the method you deposited with. Before your first request, verify your account and play your deposit through.

  1. Complete your profile. If DBBet asks for ID, proof of address or proof of your payment method, send them early.
  2. Bet at least the full amount you deposited, once, at odds of 1.1 or higher. Bets on both sides, such as red and black on roulette, do not count.
  3. Open Withdraw in the cashier and choose the same wallet you deposited with.
  4. Enter the amount and confirm the request. The minimum and maximum are shown in the cashier.
  5. Wait for the request to be processed; the money goes to your mobile wallet.

Withdrawal rules

  • Same method: money goes back to the method and number you deposited with. If you used several methods, the withdrawal is split in proportion to your deposits.
  • Timing: requests are accepted 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and can take up to 7 business days depending on the method.
  • Verification (KYC): document checks take up to 72 hours after DBBet receives them. Send withdrawal documents within one month of the request, or it can be put on hold.

Cashing out at a mobile-money agent can cost your network's usual fee and the government levy; that is not a DBBet fee. For tax, try the stake excise duty calculator.

Can I bet without downloading the app?

Requests are accepted 24/7 and can take up to 7 business days, depending on the method. Document checks take up to 72 hours from receipt. The money goes back to the method you deposited with.
