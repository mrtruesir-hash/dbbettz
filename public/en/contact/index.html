When you message support

To help us sort things out quickly, please include:

  • The phone number or username on your DBBet account.
  • What happened, and when (date and time).
  • For payment issues: the mobile-money transaction code and amount.

Never share your password with anyone. DBBet staff will never ask for your full password or mobile-money PIN.
