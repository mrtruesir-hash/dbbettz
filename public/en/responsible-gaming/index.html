Responsible gaming

Gambling should be fun — a form of entertainment, never a way to make money or fix money problems. DBBet is for adults 18 and over only. This page explains how to stay in control and where to get help.

Play safe: the basics

  • Set a budget before you play and never bet money you can't afford to lose.
  • Never chase losses. Trying to win back what you've lost usually makes it worse.
  • Set a time limit and take regular breaks.
  • Don't gamble when you're upset, stressed, or under the influence of alcohol.
  • Keep it separate from money for rent, food, school fees or bills.
  • It's not income. Treat any winnings as a bonus, not a plan.

Warning signs

Gambling may be becoming a problem if you:

  • Spend more money or time than you meant to.
  • Borrow money or sell things to gamble.
  • Lie to family or friends about how much you gamble.
  • Feel anxious, guilty or low after playing.
  • Gamble to escape problems or to win back losses.

If any of these sound familiar, it may be time to take a break or ask for help.

Tools to stay in control

Within your DBBet account you can use the following controls:

  • Deposit limits — cap how much you can deposit per day, week or month.
  • Time-out — lock yourself out for a short cooling-off period (e.g. 24 hours to a few weeks).
  • Self-exclusion — close access to your account for a longer period (six months or more) when you need a real break.
  • Reality checks — reminders of how long you've been playing.

To set any of these up, contact our team via the contact page and we'll help you put them in place.

Protecting under-18s

It is illegal for anyone under 18 to gamble. If children have access to your device, use device-level parental controls and never leave your account logged in. Keep your password and mobile-money PIN private.

Where to get help

Tanzania does not currently have a dedicated national gambling helpline. If gambling is affecting you or someone you know, you can get help through these trusted international support services, alongside your local mental-health services:

  • GamCare — gamcare.org.uk. Advice and support on gambling-related harm, including free online help.
  • Gamblers Anonymous — gamblersanonymous.org. Peer-support groups for people who want to stop gambling.
  • BeGambleAware — begambleaware.org. Information, self-assessment tools and free, confidential advice.
  • Local mental-health services — speak to a doctor, nurse or counsellor at your nearest health facility. You can also talk to someone you trust — a friend or family member. You don't have to deal with it alone.

18+ only. Gambling can be addictive. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact emergency services right away.

Need a break now?

You can pause or close your account at any time. Reach out through our contact page and ask for a time-out or self-exclusion — no questions, no pressure.
