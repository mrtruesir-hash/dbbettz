Questions

Account, verification and complaints

These answers come from DBBet's official terms and conditions.

Can I have more than one DBBet account?
No. DBBet allows one account per person and per household, which means one address, email, IP, card or wallet. If extra accounts are found, bets can be voided, bonuses and winnings cancelled, and the accounts blocked. Do not register again under another name, and do not sell or give your account to anyone.
Why has my DBBet account been blocked?
One common reason is inactivity: an account with no login and no bets for 3 months counts as inactive and can be blocked. DBBet tries to contact you first, and there is no inactivity fee. To have it unblocked, contact Support at info-en@db-bet-team.com or the Security team at security@db-bet-team.com.
How long does account verification take?
Up to 72 hours after DBBet receives your documents. DBBet may ask for ID, proof of address or proof of your payment method, and sometimes a video call, with the result given within 5 working days. If documents are requested for a withdrawal, send them within 1 month, or the withdrawal can be put on hold.
How do I make a complaint to DBBet?
Complain within 6 months of the incident, using DBBet's complaint form (PDF) sent to info-en@db-bet-team.com. If you cannot find the form, ask Support to send it. DBBet acknowledges your complaint within 7 days and gives a final answer within 4 weeks, a period it can extend once by another 4 weeks. If you are still not satisfied, you can take the matter to ADR Curaçao for free through the seal in DBBet's website footer. For casino bets, the casino rules mention a 10-day limit, so complain as early as you can.
What responsible-gaming tools does DBBet have?
Cooling-off for 24 hours, 7 days, 1 month or 3 months, for one type of game or for all of them. Self-exclusion for 1, 3, 5 or 10 years, or for life, which cannot be ended early. Deposit limits per day, week or month: lowering a limit works at once, raising one takes 7 days. A timer also shows how long your session has lasted while you are logged in.
Why can't I withdraw my money?
Check three rules. Withdrawals go to the same method you deposited with, in your own name. Your total stakes must at least equal your total deposits, at odds of 1.1 or more. And verification may be needed first. Requests are processed 24/7 and can take up to 7 business days depending on the method.
How do I close my DBBet account?
Ask Support to close it. The remaining eligible balance is paid out after verification. If you only want a break, use cooling-off or self-exclusion instead.
