How betting works on DBBet

The steps are the same in the app and on the mobile site. In short:

  1. Register with DBBet and log in to your account.
  2. Open Sports or Live. Sports lists upcoming matches; Live lists matches being played right now.
  3. Pick a sport, then a match, for example football and an NBC Premier League fixture.
  4. Tap the odds you want. The selection goes onto your bet slip.
  5. Choose the bet type if you have more than one selection: single, accumulator (mkeka) or system.
  6. Enter your stake and tap Place a bet.
  7. Track your bet in your account under Bet history. If it wins, the money is added to your balance automatically.

Careful: DBBet has a one-click bet option. When it is switched on, every tap on the odds places a bet straight away, with no confirmation. If you are just starting out, leave it off.

For most Tanzanian bettors, it starts with football. DBBet's football section has local and international matches, both pre-match and live.

NBC Premier League matches, including those of Simba SC, Young Africans (Yanga) and Azam FC, are in the football section. These three clubs have represented Tanzania in CAF club competitions, including the CAF Champions League. When those matches are on DBBet's schedule, you bet on them the same way as on any other fixture.

We don't give tips or predictions for these matches. We only explain how the markets work; the decision is yours.

Markets you will see most

  • 1X2: home win, draw or away win.
  • Double chance: two of the three results.
  • Both teams to score (GG/NG): yes or no.
  • Over/Under: total goals above or below a line, such as 2.5.
  • Handicap, including European handicap.
  • Correct score and team to score first.
