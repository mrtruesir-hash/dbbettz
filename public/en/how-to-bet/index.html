Step 3: Understand the odds

Odds tell you two things: how much you can win, and roughly how likely an outcome is. DBBet uses decimal odds, which are easy to read:

  • Odds 2.00 — a 1,000 TZS bet returns 2,000 TZS (1,000 TZS profit).
  • Odds 1.50 — a 1,000 TZS bet returns 1,500 TZS (500 TZS profit). More likely to win, smaller reward.
  • Odds 4.00 — a 1,000 TZS bet returns 4,000 TZS (3,000 TZS profit). Less likely, bigger reward.

Simple formula: potential return = stake × odds, and profit = return − stake. These examples only show the maths.

Step 4: Pick a market

A "market" is what you're betting on. In football the most common ones are 1X2, double chance, both teams to score (GG/NG), over/under goals, handicaps and correct score. Each one is explained below, with an NBC Premier League example, under bet types. If you are starting out, stick to markets you understand, like 1X2 or Over/Under.

Step 5: Single bet or accumulator (mkeka)?

  • Single bet — one selection. It wins or loses on its own. Lower risk.
  • Accumulator (mkeka) — several selections in one bet. The odds multiply for a bigger payout, but every selection must win. Higher risk, higher reward. The mkeka is very popular with football fans in Tanzania.

There is also the system bet, which splits an accumulator into smaller ones. All three are compared, with examples, in single, mkeka or system.

Markets: what you bet on

1X2

Match result

1 is a home win, X is a draw, 2 is an away win.

Example: Azam FC vs Simba SC: pick 2 and you win if Simba win.

1X · 12 · X2

Double chance

You cover two of the three results, at lower odds than 1X2.

Example: Young Africans vs Azam FC: pick X2 and you win if Azam win or it is a draw.

GG / NG

Both teams to score

GG: both teams score. NG: at least one team does not. Also called BTTS.

Example: Simba SC vs Young Africans ends 1-1, so GG wins; if it ends 2-0, NG wins.

O/U

Over/Under goals

Total goals by both teams, above (Over) or below (Under) a line.

Example: Azam FC vs Young Africans ends 2-1 (3 goals), so Over 2.5 wins.

+ / −

Handicap

One team gets virtual goals added or taken away before the result is compared. In a European handicap, a draw after the handicap is a third option.

Example: Simba SC (-1) vs Azam FC: a 2-0 result wins the bet (it becomes 1-0); a 1-0 result does not.

CS

Correct score

You predict the exact final score. It is harder, which is why the odds are high.

Example: Young Africans vs Simba SC: pick 1-0 and you only win if it ends 1-0.

Single, mkeka or system

Single

Single bet

One selection on one slip. It wins or loses on its own.

Example: Over 1.5 on Azam FC vs Simba SC, and nothing else.

Mkeka

Accumulator

Several selections on one slip. The odds multiply (1.50 × 1.80 × 2.00 = 5.40), but every selection must win.

Example: 1 on Simba SC vs Young Africans, GG on Azam FC vs Simba SC and Over 1.5 on Young Africans vs Azam FC: if one fails, the whole slip loses.

System

System bet

An accumulator split into smaller ones. In a 2/3 system, 3 selections become 3 doubles, and your stake is shared between them.

Example: Simba SC, Young Africans and Azam FC to win their matches: if one fails, one of the three doubles can still pay.

DBBet also offers other slip types, such as lucky, patent, chain, multibet, conditional and anti-accumulator. Get comfortable with these three basics before trying the others.

Frequently asked questions

How do odds work in betting?
Odds show your potential return. At odds of 2.00, a 1,000 TZS bet returns 2,000 TZS (your 1,000 TZS stake plus 1,000 TZS profit). Higher odds mean a bigger potential win but a lower chance of winning.
What is an accumulator (mkeka)?
An accumulator, known locally as a mkeka, combines several selections into one bet. The odds multiply for a bigger potential payout, but every selection must win for the bet to pay out.
What is the minimum bet in Tanzania?
The minimum and maximum are shown in the cashier. The maximum stake is set per event. Start small, and only bet what you can afford to lose.
What does 1X2 mean?
1X2 is the match-result market: 1 is a home win, X is a draw and 2 is an away win. In Simba SC vs Young Africans, 1 means Simba win, X means a draw and 2 means Yanga win.
What is a handicap in betting?
A handicap gives one team virtual goals, added or taken away, before the result is compared. With Simba SC (-1), a 2-0 Simba win becomes 1-0 after the handicap, so the bet wins; a 1-0 Simba win does not win the bet.
What does both teams to score mean?
Both teams to score (GG) means each team scores at least one goal, for example 1-1 or 2-1. NG is the opposite: at least one team does not score.
