What is Aviator?

Aviator is a crash game in the DBBet casino. Each round, a small plane takes off and a multiplier climbs: 1.20x, 1.50x, 2x and up. Press Cash Out at any moment to get your stake times the current multiplier. If the plane flies away first, that round's stake is lost.

Nobody knows when the plane will leave. Aviator is provably fair: each round's result comes from a cryptographic system that can be checked, so no person and no app can know the crash point in advance.

How to play Aviator, step by step

  1. Log in to DBBet. Use the app or the website in your phone's browser. No account yet? Register first.
  2. Open Aviator. Go to Slots (called "Nafasi" on the Swahili version of DBBet), choose the Crash category or the Aviator provider, then open the game.
  3. Place your Aviator bet. Type an amount in the bet panel and press the bet button (Bet) before the next round starts.
  4. Watch the multiplier. As the plane climbs, the multiplier and the value of your bet rise together.
  5. Cash out. Press Cash Out before the plane flies away to get your stake × the multiplier at that moment. Miss it, and the stake is lost.

A quick example

Cash out at 1.50x and you get 1.5 times your stake. Wait for 3.00x and you get 3 times. The longer you wait, the bigger the risk: the plane can leave at any moment, even very early.

Auto cash-out

Switch on auto cash-out in the bet panel and set a multiplier, for example 1.80x. Your bet cashes out by itself when the multiplier gets there. If the plane leaves first, the stake is lost as usual.

Auto bet

Auto bet repeats the same stake every round. It is handy, but your balance can drain fast. Pair it with auto cash-out, check your balance often and switch it off when you take a break.

The game also shows live stats from past rounds. They can't tell you how the next round ends: every round stands on its own.
