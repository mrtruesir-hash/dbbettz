Betting tax in Tanzania explained

Two taxes affect Tanzanian bettors: withholding tax on your winnings, and an excise duty on the money you stake. Here's how each one works, in plain language.

Please note: This is general information for players, not tax or legal advice. Betting tax rates in Tanzania are set by law (the Gaming Act and the annual Finance Act) and can change. Always confirm the current rates with the Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA) or a qualified adviser.

1. Withholding tax on your winnings

Tanzania charges a withholding tax on gambling winnings. It is deducted from your winnings (not your stake) before the payout reaches you, so what you withdraw is already net of this tax. You don't file this yourself; it's handled by the operator.

How much: Withholding tax on winnings is charged under section 31A of the Gaming Act. Published sources disagree on the exact percentage, and the rate has been changed before — so we deliberately do not quote a figure here. Confirm the current rate with the TRA or a qualified adviser before relying on it.

Example (illustrative only): if the withholding rate is 20% and you win TZS 100,000 in profit, about TZS 20,000 is withheld and around TZS 80,000 reaches your account. (Figures are illustrative — confirm the exact rate.)

2. Excise duty on your stake

Alongside this, an excise duty on the amount you stake — every bet you place — is scheduled. Once in force, it is deducted automatically from the stake before your bet is placed, so a small part of each bet goes to tax before the game starts. You don't file this yourself; it's handled by the operator.

How much: An excise duty on betting stakes has been reported, but we have not been able to confirm either the rate or whether it is currently in force. Check the position with the TRA before relying on any figure.

What this means for you

  • You don't usually file anything. Both taxes are typically deducted at source by the operator, so you don't have to submit them yourself.
  • "Winnings" means profit. Withholding tax applies to the amount you win, not the stake you already paid.
  • Your withdrawal is already net of tax. The amount you cash out has had withholding tax applied.
  • Stake excise (if enacted) is taken from the stake. A small part of each bet goes to tax before the game starts.
  • Rates change. Tanzania's betting taxes have been adjusted several times — check the current figures before relying on them.
For context: Beyond the player-facing taxes, the operator also pays tax on its side, charged on gross gaming revenue (GGR). This is a company tax, not something you deduct yourself.

Bet smart, whatever the tax

Tax is just one part of the maths. The bigger factors in the long run are staying disciplined, understanding odds and markets, and only betting what you can afford to lose. See our responsible gaming guide.

Where to check official rates

For the current, official position, refer to the Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA), the Gaming Board of Tanzania (GBT) and the latest Finance Act. If you have a complex situation, speak to a qualified tax adviser.
