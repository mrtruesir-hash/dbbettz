# DBBETZ.COM — Content Roadmap

**As of 2026-08-20.** The real, evidence-based finding across Parts 4 and 9: this site's genuinely open content ground is narrower than a typical content-roadmap template assumes, and most of the real opportunity is in sharpening 3 existing pages, not building new ones. This roadmap reflects that finding rather than padding itself with speculative new pages.

## Core topics (evidence-backed, keep investing here)

1. **Getting Started / Onboarding** — the single strongest real opportunity found this entire engagement: the Swahili query pattern "jinsi ya kujisajili dbbet tanzania" / "jinsi ya kuweka pesa dbbet vodacom" is proven to work in this niche (other operators rank for equivalent patterns) but is completely unclaimed for DBBet specifically, including by the clone-domain swarm. `/how-to-start/` already exists and was sharpened in Part 8; this remains the top page to keep evolving as real query data comes in.
2. **Account & Transaction Support** — real, validated by GSC (the site's actual best-performing page is a support post). Moderate competition from generic clone-swarm content that lacks real depth — DBBETZ can out-specify it with exact error conditions and resolution paths, not just existing.
3. **Betting Tax — practical framing** — narrowly scoped to the calculator/net-payout angle, not the general news story (which national outlets already own). The `/betting-tax/` calculator is this site's strongest linkable asset (see `DBBETZ-AUTHORITY-ROADMAP.md`).
