# DBBETZ.COM — Digital PR Campaign

**Date:** 2026-08-20 | One campaign, built on real, independently-verifiable data. No invented statistics, no invented "trend" claims.

## Headline

**"What Tanzania's New 5% Betting Tax Actually Costs You — A Free Calculator Breaks It Down"**

(Working headline for pitch use — publications will write their own if they run the story; this is a starting point, not a mandate.)

## Story Angle

Tanzania's 2026/27 government budget introduced a real, new 5% excise duty on the value of every betting stake, effective 1 July 2026 — coverage so far (Daily News, iGaming Business, Focus Gaming News, and others) has been at the policy level: what the tax is, why it exists, how much revenue it's projected to raise. Nobody has covered the practical, "what does this mean for my actual bet" angle for ordinary Tanzanian bettors. DBBETZ built a free, no-signup calculator that answers that second question directly.

## Key Finding

Not a proprietary statistic — a **transparent, checkable calculation**: on a stake of TZS 10,000, approximately TZS 500 goes to the new excise duty before the bet is even placed, leaving TZS 9,500 actually staked. This is presented as a worked example of the real, government-set rate, not as original DBBETZ research data.

## Supporting Data (all independently sourced, none DBBETZ-original)

- **5% excise duty rate**, effective 1 July 2026 — Tanzania's 2026/27 government budget, cross-confirmed by Tanzania's own Daily News and multiple independent igaming trade-press sources.
- **Real government revenue projection**: the new tax is expected to raise TZS 74.5 billion (~$28.4 million) — a real, publicly reported figure from Tanzania's Ministry of Finance, cited here as external context with clear attribution, never presented as DBBETZ's own finding.
- **10% of that revenue** is earmarked for the Gaming Board of Tanzania specifically to improve regulation and address gambling-addiction consequences — also a real, independently reported detail, not DBBETZ's claim.

## Methodology

The calculator performs one transparent calculation: `excise duty = stake × 5%`; `net staked = stake − excise duty`. The rate and effective date are both cited on-page to Tanzania's 2026/27 government budget and Tanzania's Daily News. No hidden assumptions, no proprietary data, no black-box scoring.

## Limitations (stated plainly, including in any pitch)

- The calculator does **not** model the separate withholding tax on winnings — that rate is genuinely unconfirmed (published sources disagree, and it has changed before), so DBBETZ deliberately built that half of the tool as a user-input estimator rather than assert a number nobody can verify. Any journalist should know this distinction going in.
- This is a single, simple calculation (stake × rate), not a predictive model or original research study — it should be described accurately as a transparency tool, not "DBBETZ research."
- Tax rates can change; the tool states its rate is current as of publication and should be re-verified with the TRA for anything beyond illustrative use.
