## Success measures (not a link quota)

Response rate, positive response rate, actual placements, relevant referring domains, editorial links, referral traffic where measurable, brand mentions, branded-search growth (trackable via GSC). No fixed target number of links — a real, quality-first placement from one A-tier prospect is worth more than volume from the spam pattern already documented in the current backlink profile.

## What this roadmap does NOT include

- Mass outreach or link buying — explicitly against this engagement's standing rules.
- Any prospect not independently verified live and legitimate.
- A second campaign asset — support-post content was evaluated and found to have near-zero external linkability; the calculator remains the only real linkable asset identified.
